About Me

Daojun Bi (1994–) holds a Ph.D. in Economics and is a faculty member at Xinyang Normal University. Her research interests lie in Environmental and Development Economics, Labor Resource Allocation, and Economic Statistics. Her research has been published in prestigious journals, including Urban Problems, Economic Latitude, Exploring Economic Issues, and the Polish Journal of Environmental Studies, Politics, Economy, and Industry. Dr. Bi has served as the Principal Investigator (PI) for two provincial-level research projects and multiple departmental-level projects, and has contributed to several national-level projects funded by the National Social Science Fund and the Ministry of Education.
